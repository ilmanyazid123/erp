   257→## 6. Typography
   258→
   259→- **Font stack (body, headings, buttons):** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` (Tailwind v4 default stack). The site **loads Roboto** via Google Fonts (`Roboto:ital,wght@0,100..900;1,100..900`) as the primary intended typeface, but the CSS stack falls back through system fonts.
   260→- **Weights available (Roboto):** 100, 300, 400, 500, 700, 900 (+ italics).
   261→- **Heading sizes (desktop):**
   262→  - H1 (hero): `60px`, `font-weight: 600` (semibold), white
   263→  - H2 (section titles): `~30px` (text-3xl), `font-weight: 500` (medium), `text-foreground` (dark navy)
   264→  - H3 (card titles): smaller, `font-semibold`
   265→- **Body text:** `text-foreground/70` or `/80` (dark navy at 70–80% opacity), `text-sm` (14px) for descriptions, regular weight.
   266→- **Buttons / nav:** `text-sm` (14px), `font-weight: 500` (medium).
   267→- **Tracking:** `tracking-normal` (no exaggerated letter-spacing).
   268→- No serif fonts anywhere — fully sans-serif, modern, utility-first aesthetic.
